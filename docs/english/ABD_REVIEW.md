The ABD lineage is initial 900 → correction 176 (116 targets and 60 controls) → independent review of 31 disagreement controls → final 900. These records belong only to ABD; historical R1/R3/GenS IDs with the same E-number are different identities.

The independent result was frozen before identity/label association. It agrees with the original ABD review on 5/31 and the correction review on 16/31; all three labels differ on 10/31. Twenty records carry independent review flags. Independent labels comprise 5 supported, 6 partially supported, 12 unsupported, 8 contradicted and 0 unreviewable.

These controls were selected for disagreement. Their rates do not estimate agreement or error across all 900 records. The diagnostic preserves old/new labels without adjudication. It distinguishes label-boundary differences from evidence-interpretation differences, and compares question/option/prediction/reason identity, map bytes, image hashes/order and resolved timestamps. Matching source material does not prove identical on-screen presentation; item-level display layout/order was not fully retained.

Typical distinctions include partial contextual clues versus support for every component of a compound option; sparse snapshots versus duration/frequency evidence; a later event versus the immediate next event; and missing option images versus a directly verifiable selection. The per-case English rationales in the original comparison provide the independent judgments; Chinese explanations and case-level diagnostic commentary remain frozen, not relabelled by this companion.

The final precedence rule selects independent review over correction over original ABD, yielding 31 + 145 + 724 = 900 records. This is not a fresh independent review of all 900 and does not establish complete reviewer agreement.
