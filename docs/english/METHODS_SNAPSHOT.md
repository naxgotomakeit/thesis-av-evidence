The audit inspected local source, configuration, manifests and archive listings without running experiments, models or APIs. Its missing-source statements describe what was available on that machine at that time; they are not a current repository-wide coverage assessment following the School imports.

The report could not establish the formal R1 category-distribution estimator, normalization/smoothing, handling of multiple same-class detections, empty distributions, JSD boundary conditions or their relationship to label summaries. It deliberately supplied no inferred formulas.

It also lacked accepted local/Planner-only and Full Staged paired-100 bindings. Fine candidate sets, score weights, region reselection, deduplication keys, Shared/Fine/Final transitions and retry-versus-inspection rules therefore remained unconfirmed in that snapshot. A prompt instruction alone does not prove controller enforcement.

Similar auxiliary code was explicitly excluded: `fine_reranking_v1` belonged to a single-video/six-question setting, and separate Medium retrieval weights did not establish the formal Staged Fine-ranking formula. VideoSEAL Dense-H and Direct source could not substitute for Staged source identity.

The report requested accepted builder/controller snapshots, configurations, prompts, population and input manifests, and SHA/lineage bindings before writing implementation-specific claims.
